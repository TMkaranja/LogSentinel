**Purpose:** resolve the source-publication status of the 23 GUI artwork/resource files and specify the minimum follow-up needed for a complete GUI source, test, and asset set.

**Disposition:** evidence review only. This report does not establish asset ownership, grant rights, constitute a legal opinion, authorize staging/publication, or clear a binary. No GUI asset, GUI source, test, packaging specification, licensing file, or release artifact was modified. The Windows GUI EXE and ZIP remain **HOLD — NOT CLEARED FOR DISTRIBUTION**.

**Snapshot reviewed:** `main` at `3229b17f9f0ae4fefc48e58f7a688f9250e1a117`; `origin/main` at `e5bcfd0d78cdb520025ff2cc2398aaaedb1fada0`. Local `main` is one commit ahead. The pre-existing tracked changes to `.gitignore`, `README.md`, `docs/USAGE.md`, and `packaging/LogSentinel-GUI-Onedir.spec` were preserved. No paths were staged. Twenty local `refs/t3/orchestration-v2/checkpoints/*` refs were present; they were not changed. The existing ignored `release/` directory and EXE/ZIP were preserved.

- **23/23 assets reviewed:** 22 SVGs and `logsentinel.ico`. Their complete working-tree byte sizes and SHA-256 values are recorded in `docs/PHASE-1-GUI-ASSET-PROVENANCE-REPORT.md`; this review reconfirmed the current asset paths, source history and upstream icon comparisons. ### Classification meanings

- **Verified original artwork:** creator and source/design history are established by adequate item-specific records. No asset met this category.
- **Verified third-party artwork with identifiable license:** historical upstream source/revision for the local asset is established and the applicable terms can be assigned to that source. No asset met this category; geometric matching alone is insufficient.
- **Generated artwork with documented provenance:** the input/source, generator or authoring process, output mapping, and applicable rights are documented. No asset met this category. Outlined lettering and PNG-backed ICO frames are not, on their own, proof of generated provenance.
- **Probable source; historical origin unverified:** a specific upstream source is strongly supported by exact geometry, but historical adoption/source and local rights basis are not established. Four assets meet this evidence description.
- **Unknown provenance:** no source/author/rights chain is established. Nineteen assets meet this description.

These are evidence classifications, not judgments of infringement or permission. “HOLD” is a publication control, not a conclusion that the asset cannot be used.

### Minimum information needed to exit the asset HOLD
